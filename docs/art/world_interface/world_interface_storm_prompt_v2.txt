Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a new visual redesign proposal for the Minecraft boss "WORLD INTERFACE", strongly inspired by the Wither Storm from Minecraft Story Mode. The previous design was rejected because it resembled a small three-headed floating stone animal. This must instead feel like a colossal sky-blocking world-devouring disaster.

Wide cinematic concept board. Main image occupies left 75 percent: low player-eye perspective from a Minecraft End island, looking up at an enormous black Wither Storm creature hanging overhead. Small obsidian towers and a single tiny Minecraft player establish overwhelming scale. Voxel/block-built game-art aesthetic, crisp cuboid forms, deliberately textured pixel surfaces, stylized 3D concept render. A mountainous bulbous irregular dark mass occupies the upper image, NOT a flat floating island, rock spaceship, turtle shell or horizontal stack of slate. Surface is densely fused obsidian-black voxel flesh and chunky irregular accretions, with torn terrain embedded within it. Keep its large silhouette readable. THREE AND ONLY THREE recognizably Wither-like block skull heads emerge on short thick roots from the FRONT and lower front of the huge body: central head largest, two flanking heads smaller and offset. Heads have wide gaping rectangular jaws, dark block teeth, intense violet eyes and interior mouths. Head-to-body proportion much smaller than previous hydra concept, so the body feels overwhelming. NO upright snake necks, NO long exposed swan necks. Long massive irregular segmented black tentacles hang far downward and curl toward terrain; varied lengths, thick roots tapering into angular tips, some foreground and some behind. One central violet suction beam from a mouth draws a sparse clear spiral of Minecraft End-stone cubes and larger chunks up from the ground; environmental debris follows the vortex, never resembles random confetti. Body is readable even in darkness, use restrained lavender rim lighting, dark storm atmosphere and pale distant End horizon. Violet concentrated in eyes, mouths, beam and a few deep fissures, not all-over neon. No chest eye, no halo, no wings, no humanoid torso, no legs. No gore.

Right 25 percent is a tidy vertical design strip on warm light gray background separated from scene by thin line. Three miniature full-body silhouette/concept studies of THE SAME CREATURE labeled exactly "01 / EMERGENCE", "02 / DEVOURING", "03 / WORLD STORM". Each study retains exactly three heads embedded toward front and drooping tentacles. Stage 1 is a smaller compact black mass, stage 2 swells and grows longer grasping tentacles, stage 3 is a vast asymmetric storm body with ruptured voxel terrain accretions. Clearly escalating scale and disaster, not three different species. Include tiny player marker beside each. These are silhouette and mood explorations, NOT strict orthographic modeling diagrams. Bottom unobtrusive title "WORLD INTERFACE / STORM STUDY 02". Production-quality concept artwork, strong silhouette, awe and horror, Minecraft visual language, no watermark.
